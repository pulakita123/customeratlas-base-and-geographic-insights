# Business Problem & Analysis Objectives

## Business context
A customer list can tell a business who is in its database, but a simple total does not explain how the customer base is changing, how long customers have been associated with the business, or whether customers are concentrated in particular countries or organisations.

This project uses a customer-level dataset and an Excel dashboard to explore those questions.

## Problem statement
**How can customer data be transformed into a clear, interactive report that helps decision-makers understand customer growth, tenure and customer-base concentration?**

## Business questions
1. How many customers are represented in the dataset?
2. How does customer acquisition vary by subscription year?
3. What does the distribution of customer tenure look like?
4. Which countries contribute the largest customer counts?
5. Which companies contribute the largest customer counts?
6. How concentrated or diversified is the customer base across geography and company?

## Stakeholders and their information needs

| Stakeholder | Information need | Potential use |
|---|---|---|
| Business leadership | Overall customer-base size and trend | Monitor the scale and direction of the customer base |
| Sales / account teams | Company-level customer distribution | Understand where accounts are concentrated |
| Market / regional teams | Country-level distribution | Explore geographic coverage and potential concentration |
| Customer success | Tenure distribution | Identify segments for further retention investigation |
| Data / reporting team | Consistent definitions and validated totals | Maintain reliable recurring reporting |

These are likely stakeholder groups for the analytical scenario, not people interviewed for this project.

## Analysis objectives
- Establish a consistent customer count.
- Describe changes in customer additions by subscription year.
- Summarise tenure using a clearly defined calculation and grouping.
- Compare customer distribution by country and company.
- Present the results in a dashboard that can be filtered by year, country and company.

## Important boundaries
This is descriptive customer-base analysis. Without revenue, cost, churn, satisfaction or transaction data, the project cannot determine profitability, customer lifetime value, churn rate, customer satisfaction or the reasons behind observed trends.
